1. Onboarding
   When you first signed up, did the welcome tutorial give you a clear understanding of what the app does and how to navigate it? Was anything confusing or missing?

2. Course Creation & AI Quality
   Upload a set of real notes you actually use. Do the generated flashcards and quiz questions feel accurate and relevant to what you uploaded — or do they feel generic/off-topic?

3. Flashcard Experience
   How did studying the flashcards feel? Was the flow smooth, and did you feel like you were actually learning?

4. Quiz Experience
   After taking a quiz, were the questions appropriately challenging? Did the answer explanations help you understand why an answer was right or wrong?

5. Performance
   Did any part of the app feel noticeably slow or did anything stall completely? If so, where exactly did it happen?

6. Navigation
   Could you find everything you needed without guessing? Were there any moments where you weren't sure where to go next?

7. Gamification
   Did earning points and badges feel rewarding, or did it feel like background noise you ignored? Did you understand how points were being calculated?

8. Sharing
   Try sharing a study pack with another tester and having them import it. Did the process feel straightforward, and did the imported content look correct on their end?

9. Account & Settings
   Try updating your display name or profile photo, and attempt a password change. Did everything save correctly and reflect immediately across the app?

10. Biggest Friction Point
    What is the single most frustrating or broken thing you encountered? If you had to fix one thing before launch, what would it be?
